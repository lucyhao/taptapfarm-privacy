Tap Tap Farm — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Tap Tap Farm is a simple tap-and-play app for toddlers: tap an animal, vehicle, or dinosaur and it reacts with a sound. The short version of this policy is the whole policy — the app does not collect, store, or share any personal information, from children or adults, ever.

✓ No account or sign-up ✓ No ads ✓ No analytics or tracking ✓ No data leaves your device

Information We Collect

None. Tap Tap Farm doesn't ask for your name, email, photos, location, or any other personal information. There's no account to create and nothing to sign in to. The app has no server and makes no network requests of its own — it just runs entirely on your device.

Children's Privacy

Tap Tap Farm is designed to be used by young children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children, which means there's nothing for us to review, delete, or disclose if you ever ask. If you believe this is not the case for any reason, contact us at the email below and we'll look into it.

In-App Purchases

Zoo, Dinosaur, and Vehicles are unlocked through a single one-time purchase, processed entirely by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). Tap Tap Farm never sees or stores your payment information — Apple handles billing, your card details, and your receipt directly.

The app only remembers whether you've unlocked that content, and it stores that single yes/no flag locally on your device. It's never sent anywhere, and reinstalling the app or restoring purchases simply asks Apple to confirm it again.

Advertising & Analytics

Tap Tap Farm shows no ads and includes no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs of any kind.

Data Security

Since nothing is collected and nothing leaves your device, there's no data of yours sitting on a server anywhere for us to secure, lose, or have breached.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that does involve data — we'll update this page and change the "Last updated" date above.
